# Communications and interviews

Open an opportunity’s **Interviews & comms** tab and choose **Add activity**. Record as many communications, interviews or assessments as needed.

- Communications have optional incoming/outgoing direction and a channel, including custom names such as LinkedIn or a recruiter portal. Older entries remain visibly without a recorded direction.
- Interview type/format accepts a suggested value or your own label. Final-stage interviews keep their existing marker.
- Status can be planned, rescheduled, completed or cancelled. To move a meeting, edit its date and choose rescheduled; the earlier date remains in expandable revision history. Dates/times use the device’s timezone on entry and the workspace’s timezone for display.
- Follow-up text has an optional due date and completion checkbox. Pending follow-ups join the dashboard and to-do list in due-date order, even after a completed conversation/interview. Complete the follow-up by editing its source activity. Cancelled activities and closed opportunities do not contribute pending follow-ups. These are derived actions, not duplicate saved tasks; completed follow-ups remain on their activity rather than the completed-task list.
- Attach existing opportunity documents or upload files in the form. Each link refers to a particular document version. Uploads use the existing document workflow and 15 MB browser limit, with up to 100 distinct attachments per activity. Library and other opportunities’ documents cannot be linked directly; add the intended copy to the opportunity first.
- Unlinking an attachment does not delete its original. If an original is deleted from Documents, its old activity link remains labelled unavailable and can be removed. Uploads that succeeded before an activity-save error remain in Documents; retrying a pending upload uses its original request identifier to avoid duplicates.

Edits retain previous activity fields, document IDs and the correction recording time. Existing limited historical snapshots stay as recorded. The combined Summary includes direction/channel, follow-ups and attachment names. Activity metadata travels with record exports and full backups; record JSON exports do not contain file bytes.

Career Buddy’s existing reviewed activity proposals remain compatible. New fields are manually editable in this release. This feature records communications and follow-ups; it does not send email, create calendar events or schedule notifications.
